The Market

Big, fragmented, and wide open.

The US roofing market is worth roughly $34.7B in 2026, growing ~6% a year, with the top three contractors holding under 4% combined share. Replacement and renovation — the recurring, lead-driven work — is ~79% of volume. There is no consolidated technology winner serving the mid-to-large operator who owns their data.

Beachhead: mid-to-large residential & restoration roofers doing $3M+ in revenue — large enough to feel the pain, small enough to move fast.
